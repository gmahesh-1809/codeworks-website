Overview

Drishti.ai is an enterprise credit assessment and investigation platform for banks, NBFCs and lending institutions. It enables deeper, technology-led assessment of borrowers across the pre-sanction and post-sanction lifecycle, going beyond conventional credit checks and standard verification processes.

Built on a neuro-symbolic AI architecture, Drishti.ai combines AI reasoning with domain knowledge, business rules and deterministic controls to examine information, identify inconsistencies and surface risk indicators that may require further investigation.

No Rip and Replace of existing systems.

Drishti.ai brings intelligent investigation and credit assessment into the core of modern lending.

Four Intelligent Agents

  1. 01

    Document Verification Agent

    Verifies and cross-validates information across borrower documents and supporting evidence, identifying discrepancies, anomalies and missing information.
  2. 02

    Investigation Agent

    Performs deeper investigations across available borrower and business information to uncover relationships, inconsistencies, risk indicators and findings relevant to the credit decision.
  3. 03

    Credit Assessment Report Agent

    Consolidates verification and investigation findings into structured credit assessment reports, providing credit teams with a comprehensive view of the case and supporting evidence.
  4. 04

    Credit Copilot

    Gives credit professionals an intelligent interface to query case information, explore findings and navigate the assessment process, keeping the human decision-maker at the centre of the credit decision.

Drishti.ai is scalable and configurable, enabling institutions to configure investigation parameters, business rules, workflows and assessment requirements according to their lending products, policies and operating models.

By strengthening verification, investigation and assessment across the credit lifecycle, Drishti.ai helps institutions identify risk earlier, improve assessment quality, reduce manual effort and make better-informed lending decisions.
