Verified at Proposal. Evidenced at Claim.

Assay is a claims integrity and settlement platform for insurers. It tests what a proposer or claimant declared against the insurer’s own records and, with the customer’s consent, their bank and tax data, then settles what is owed.

The insurer’s officer decides every claim. Nothing is auto-rejected or auto-approved: a flagged file arrives with the reason already set out.

Lines of business: motor own damage, health, life underwriting, marine & transit, fire & burglary, and personal accident.

Calculated, Not Estimated

All arithmetic runs in a deterministic engine that shows its working, never in a model. Assay runs in the insurer’s own data centre or private cloud in India, and documents are read by an AI model the insurer approves, which can run on its own servers.

Assay brings underwriting, claims, documents, calculations, settlement and recovery into one evidenced record.

Module 01 · Claims Integrity

Tests what the proposer and the claimant declared. Nothing is auto-rejected: a flagged file goes to the officer who owns that decision.

Underwriting Checks
Declared stock, ownership and income tested against the audited accounts, the registration record, bank inflows and tax filings.
Claim Checks
Cover in force, claim timing, waiting periods and quantum, checked on every claim.
Documents in the File
A discharge summary read against the proposal, with every extracted figure cited and confirmed by the insurer’s officer.
Configurable Thresholds
Set in configuration, not code: proposed by one person, put in force by another from a set date, with the earlier figure kept.

Module 02 · Settlement & Recovery

Computes, approves and reconciles claim payments, intermediary commission and recovery. It never moves money: instructions go out, confirmations come in.

Governed Formulas
Claim, commission and recovery amounts computed from formulas the insurer’s team can read.
Authority & Countersign
Limits by amount and line of business, maker never checker, and a second signature on a large repudiation.
Balanced Subledger
Double-entry to the paisa, reconciled against bank and disbursement confirmations.
Clawback & Recovery
Commission reversal and fee recovery handled as first-class flows, not adjustments.
