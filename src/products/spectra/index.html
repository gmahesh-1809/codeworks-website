Complete Subvention Processing

Spectra automates the end-to-end processing of subvention claims—from scheme eligibility and claim tracing to rule-based calculation, reconciliation, nodal agency submission, settlement processing and credit release reporting.

Its configurable business rule engine and workflow framework manage scheme-specific eligibility, calculations, validations, claim batching, multi-source reconciliation and exceptions with complete transaction-level traceability.

Built for Scale and Change

Spectra is scalable and configurable, allowing institutions to accommodate multiple schemes, changing eligibility criteria, business rules, calculation methodologies and operational workflows without redesigning the processing engine.

Spectra brings scheme rules, claims, calculations, reconciliation, audit, submission, settlement and traceability into one controlled subvention processing platform.

01
Scheme Eligibility & Rules
Configure scheme-specific eligibility criteria, business rules, parameters and workflows.
02
Claim Tracing & Validation
Trace eligible claims across source systems, validate supporting data and identify exceptions.
03
Rule-Based Calculation
Apply 100% rule-based calculations consistently across high-volume claim populations, with every calculation traceable to the applicable scheme rules and parameters.
04
Claim Batching & Nodal Agency Submission
Generate validated claim batches and prepare them for submission to the relevant nodal agencies.
05
Audit & Certification Support
Generate comprehensive claim audit reports and supporting documentation for independent audit and certification by Big 4 audit firms, enabling the claim batch to be reviewed and certified before submission to the nodal agency.
06
Reconciliation
Reconcile claims, transactions and nodal agency responses across multiple data sources, identifying mismatches, adjustments and unresolved exceptions.
07
Settlement Processing
Establish the reconciled settlement position with a complete record of claims, calculations, adjustments and reconciliation outcomes.
08
Credit Release Reporting
Generate credit release reports from validated and reconciled settlement data for downstream processing. Spectra does not execute the actual credit release to the borrower or financial institution.
09
Traceability & Audit
Maintain an end-to-end, transaction-level trail across eligibility, rules, calculations, claims, validations, submissions, reconciliations, exceptions and settlement outcomes, providing the evidence required for audit, certification and operational review.
